Device tree blob provenance

The committed device-tree.dtb (and its copies under Resources/DTBs and the
FdtBlob referenced by pgp110.fdf) was captured from this phone's own running
Android:

  adb exec-out su -c 'cat /sys/firmware/fdt' > device-tree.dtb

It is the vendor FDT of the PGP110 unit, kept as a build input. It has not
been recompiled from a mainline tree. Re-capture it from the target unit
(PGP110 or AQ101) before porting that variant.
